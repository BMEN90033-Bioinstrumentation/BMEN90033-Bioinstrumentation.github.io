Models used by the head view of volume_conductor.html

head.glb
  Derived from "Infinite, 3D Head Scan" by Lee Perry-Smith, licensed under a
  Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported License
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Based on a work at
  www.triplegangers.com; obtained from the three.js examples
  (https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js, examples/models/gltf/LeePerrySmith).
  Changes: cropped below the neck, translated and rescaled to scene units.

brain.glb
  Derived from the pial surfaces of "Brain for Blender" by Anderson Winkler
  (https://brainder.org/brain-for-blender), licensed under a Creative Commons
  Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported License
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). brain.glb is distributed
  under the same licence.
  Changes: hemispheres merged, simplified by vertex clustering, cropped to the
  part above the section plane, rescaled and positioned inside the head scan.

head_section.json
  Scalp and brain-envelope radii in the section plane, one value per degree,
  measured from the two models above.

build_models.py
  The script that produced the three files from the source meshes:
  python3 build_models.py LeePerrySmith.glb lh.pial.obj rh.pial.obj <out dir>

The head and the brain come from different people. Their relative position is
set by hand and is illustrative, not anatomical registration.
